What Haza collects, and why.

Haza is a social driving app. It only works if it knows where you are and can carry your voice to your friends, so we collect location and audio — and as little else as possible. This page says exactly what, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it. (Written by the developer, not a lawyer; have it reviewed before launch.)

What we collect

Account. Your name and email (from Sign in with Apple — you can hide your email — or the email you sign in with), a display name, and the cars you add. We never see your Apple ID password.

Location. Your position, speed and heading while the app is open and, if you allow "Always", in the background so drives record themselves and friends can find you. Drives (route, distance, time) are stored with your account. Overnight parked positions are used only to suggest where home is; you confirm or reject the suggestion.

Voice. When you hold the Talk button your microphone audio is sent live to the people in that channel. We do not record or store talk audio.

Radar detector data. If you pair a detector, the alerts it reports (band, direction, strength, your position at the time) are shown to you and, if you turn sharing on, to your friends for 15 minutes. They are deleted after that.

Purchases. Whether you have Pro and when it expires, via Apple. We never see your payment details.

Device. A push-notification token so friends can ping you, and a random per-install identifier used only to stop referral abuse.

Who can see what

Friends you have accepted see your display name, car, position, heading and (if you allow it) speed. Inside 150 m of your confirmed home they see your home pin, never your exact position. Speed is never ranked or shown as a leaderboard. Your maximum speed for a drive is visible only to you.

Nobody else. There are no public profiles. We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone for marketing. We show no ads.

Who processes it for us

Supabase (database, sign-in, real-time location relay; servers in the United States), LiveKit (real-time audio relay for Talk), and Apple (sign-in, payments, push notifications). Each processes data only to provide the service.

How long we keep it

Live positions expire after 24 hours; shared radar alerts after 15 minutes; detailed route points after 30 days on the free plan (kept while you're a Pro member); drives, cars and friendships until you delete them or your account.

Delete everything

Profile › Delete account removes your account and all associated data immediately — drives, friendships, positions, tokens. Cancel a subscription separately in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Your choices

Location: choose "While Using" or "Always" in iOS Settings, change any time. Speed sharing and the home bubble are switches in Profile. Radar sharing is a switch on the Radar tab. Notifications: iOS Settings.

Children

Haza is for drivers. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
