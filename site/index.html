For people who drive together

See your friends on the road. Talk with one press.

Live map, walkie-talkie on iPhone, Apple Watch and CarPlay, drives you plan together, and your radar detector on the same screen.

Download on the App Store

Three things. That's the app.

The map. Friends appear as their car, with heading and (if they share it) speed. Inside 150 m of home, friends see a home pin, never your street.

Talk. Hold one button. Works from the Lock Screen, Control Center, your Watch, and the CarPlay Dashboard. Your car's play/pause button keys the mic.

Plans. Post a drive; when it starts, everyone going is on the map with their own channel.

Radar

Pair a Valentine One Gen2 over Bluetooth. Haza only displays what your detector reports. Radar detectors are illegal in passenger vehicles in Virginia, Washington D.C., on military installations, and in commercial vehicles over 10,000 lb.

Pro

Unlimited history and route playback, unlimited crews and plans, all your cars, radar alert sharing. $4.99/month or $29.99/year with a 7-day free trial, billed through Apple. Invite a friend and you both get free Pro time after their first drive.

Haza does not rank or reward speed. Obey the speed limit and the laws where you drive.
