Common questions

Talk doesn't work in the background. Join the channel from the Talk tab first (Apple requires a tap in the app), then the system walkie-talkie stays live on the Lock Screen.

Friends can't see me. Check Settings › Haza › Location. "While Using" shares only while the app is open; "Always" keeps you on the map.

Delete my account. Profile › Delete account removes everything immediately. Subscriptions are cancelled separately in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
